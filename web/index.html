Real geometry

Every printed part above is the actual mesh from hardware/Right_Hand_Parts — 17 files, 215,000 triangles, packed to 1.9 MB. The digits ship as print plates of loose phalanges and are reassembled here piece by piece. Servos, boards and electrodes are bought parts, modelled from their package dimensions.

The analog front end

From 1 millivolt to 1 volt in six stages

Surface EMG arrives buried under mains hum and electrode drift, at an amplitude a thousand times too small to digitise. Each stage does one job.
